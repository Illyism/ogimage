Used by [og.cool](https://github.com/steven-tey/og), you can display headlines directly inside your news article's OG images.

To try out **OG.cool**, all you need to do is replace the `https://` (or `https://www.`) portion of the news article's URL with `https://og.cool/`. (Only for NYT, Wired and Techcrunch):

- Original URL: https://www.nytimes.com/2023/08/23/climate/ocean-warming-fish.html
- **OG.cool** URL: https://og.cool/nytimes.com/2023/08/23/climate/ocean-warming-fish.html
